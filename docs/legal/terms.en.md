business in the app and, on the “Pro” plan, anyone who keeps the books of

individual entrepreneurs and organizations and only for their business.

## 2. How the contract is made

1. The user picks a plan on the “Buy” page and writes to the seller at
   cholakhyanerik@gmail.com: the plan, the TIN (ՀՎՀՀ) of their IE or
   organization, its name, a contact to send the key to, and the words
   “I accept the Kvartik license terms in the edition of
   November 20, 2026”.
2. The user pays for the key. The contract is made upon payment.
3. The seller sends the key to that contact within one business day after
   payment, together with the settlement document.

## 3. Trial period

The app is free to use for 14 days, with no sign-up and with every
feature. That is how you can try it before buying. The 14 days count from
the earliest of these days: the first opening on a device, the first day of
the data in the app, and the first day of the trial period for the TIN
(ՀՎՀՀ) given in the profile. Data moved with a backup to another device or
browser brings the days already used along with it, and, with your consent,
the first day for a TIN is kept by the Kvartik server (see section 2 of the
Privacy Policy): for the same TIN the trial period doesn't start over in
another browser, on another device or after the data is cleared. While the
profile has a TIN and there is no key, invoices and acts can be issued
during the trial period with this consent; without a TIN in the profile it
isn't needed. If someone other than you started the trial period for your
TIN, write to the seller: after checking, the seller will remove the
record, and the trial period will start again.

## 4. The license: what you may do and for how long

1. The key is issued to the user's TIN (ՀՎՀՀ) for the plan's term stated
   on the “Buy” page and works on any number of their devices, with no
   territorial limit. A “Standard” key opens the individual entrepreneur
   of that TIN in the app; it does not work with another TIN. A “Pro” key
   opens as many individual entrepreneurs on the “Entrepreneurs in the
   app” list as the plan has seats on the “Buy” page: the first ones, in
   the order they were added; an entrepreneur with their own valid
   “Standard” key takes no seat, and the seat goes to the next one. The
   term is counted from the day the key is issued or, if the key is paid
   for before the term of the current key of the same plan for the same
   TIN ends, from the day after the current key's last day.
2. The user may open the app in a browser, enter and keep their data in
   it, and with a “Pro” key also the data of the individual entrepreneurs
   whose books they keep, issue invoices and acts, calculate taxes, make
   exports and backups, for their own business.
3. The key may not be given, sold or rented to anyone else, and the app
   may not be passed off as one's own.
4. With a valid key every feature of the plan works. After the term ends,
   new invoices and acts cannot be issued, and the return figures for
   quarters that ended after the term cannot be opened. Everything else
   works always: the data, drafts, income and expenses, the tax on screen,
   PDFs of issued documents, export and backup. The user's data remains
   the user's.

## 5. Price and payment

The plan's price, term and number of seats are those on the “Buy” page on
the day of payment; the whole term is paid up front. Payment methods are
listed on the same page.

## 6. Refunds

Once the key has been issued, the money is not refunded: an issued key
cannot be revoked, and the app can be tried before buying during the
trial period. The seller refunds in two cases:

1. the key has not arrived within one business day after payment and the
   user asks for a refund before the key is issued: the whole amount;
2. the payment went through twice or for more than the price: the excess.

Refunds go the way the payment came or, where that is impossible, to
a bank account the user names, within five business days after the
request. If the seller's mistake issued the key to the wrong TIN, the
seller issues a key to the right TIN free of charge.

## 7. Updates

The app updates itself, and new versions, with fixes and new tax rules,
reach all users, including after the key's term has ended.

## 8. Tax calculation and liability

Kvartik calculates taxes from the text of Armenian law as the app's author
reads it; it helps with the calculation and is not tax advice (see
“About”). The user is responsible for the returns filed and the taxes
paid. The app is provided “as is”: the seller is not liable for
calculation errors, loss of data on the user's device or interruptions
of the site, except for a deliberate breach of the contract and where the
law does not allow such a disclaimer.

Answers of the AI assistant are written by the model of the provider whose
key the user connected, under that provider's terms and at the user's
expense. They are not tax advice: a model's answer can be wrong, and the
seller does not check it and is not liable for it. Tax amounts are
calculated by the app itself, not by the model.

## 10. Complaints and disputes

Complaints go to the seller at cholakhyanerik@gmail.com; the seller answers within five
business days. If no agreement is reached, the dispute is decided by
a court of the Republic of Armenia. The terms are governed by the law of
the Republic of Armenia.

## 11. Changes to the terms

A new edition is published on the site with its date and applies to keys
paid for after it. A paid key is governed by the terms of its payment day,
