## In short

Your data does not leave your device, except the TIN (ՀՎՀՀ) of your IE if
you agreed to have it checked while you have no key (section 2), and the
questions you ask the AI assistant if you connected your own AI provider's
key to it: the app sends them straight to that provider, not through us
(section 2). Kvartik has no accounts, no analytics, no counters and no ads.

## 1. Who is responsible

Individual entrepreneur Erik Cholakhyan (TIN (ՀՎՀՀ) 58111423, Yerevan) is responsible for it; the address for letters is cholakhyanerik@gmail.com.

## 2. Data in the app

The IE's details, clients, invoices and acts, income and expenses, files
attached to entries and the license key are kept only in the browser on your
device. The app sends them to no one, neither to us nor to third parties,
except the TIN for the trial period and what you ask the AI assistant
yourself (below). This rests on more than a promise: the site's security
settings forbid the app to contact any address other than kvartik.com and
the four addresses at which the AI assistant asks AI providers.

The trial period is also counted by the TIN (License Terms, section 3),
with your consent. While the profile has a TIN and there is no key for it,
the app asks for consent once, before the first invoice or act is issued
and on the license screen, with words about the same things as here and an
“I agree” button. Without consent the TIN goes nowhere, but invoices and
acts can't be issued during the trial period; without a TIN in the profile
or with a key no consent is needed. Consent is given by the IE with this
TIN or by someone the IE has asked to.

With consent, the app, when it opens but no more than once an hour, sends
the TIN to kvartik.com, and the Kvartik server answers from which day the
trial period has been running for it; the first such question records that
day. This way the trial period doesn't start over in another browser, on
another device or after the data is cleared. The server keeps not the TIN
itself but its fingerprint (HMAC-SHA256 with the server's secret key:
without the key the number can't be learned from it), the first day of the
trial period, the time of the first question and the version of the
consent words, with no IP address, nothing else about you and no data from
the app, and deletes them 3 years after the first day of the trial period.
Someone who knows your TIN can ask the server the same way and learn whether
a trial period was started for this TIN and when. You can withdraw consent
on the license screen (“Withdraw consent”) or by writing to the seller: the
record on the server is deleted (on a letter,
within 10 business days at the latest), and issuing during the trial period
asks for consent again.

The AI assistant works only if you connected to it an API key of your own
account with one of the AI providers: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT),
Google (Gemini) or xAI (Grok). The key is kept only in the browser on this
device and does not go into backups. When you ask a question, the app sends
to that provider, straight from the browser and with your key: your
question, the earlier questions and answers of the same conversation,
today's date, the window language and Kvartik's reference — its tax rules
with their sources. None of this passes through the Kvartik server, and we
do not see it. The question carries no other data from the app — TIN, name,
clients, amounts, entries — unless you type it in yourself: the “Add my
details” button puts the regime, activity codes, registration day,
citizenship and residence status from the profile into the question field,
where you see them and can delete them before sending. The app does not keep
conversations: “New conversation” or a closed window erases them. The
provider also receives your IP address and browser details, processes the
question under its own terms and privacy policy, which you accepted when you
got the key, and charges you for the answers at its own prices. All four
providers are US companies (section 3). You can remove the key on the AI
assistant screen.

The app also keeps its settings on the device: the language, the day the
trial period started, the day of the last backup, a check value of the
backup password (not the password itself), the auto-backup folder and key if
you turned it on, the list of your IEs if there are several, which version
you have already seen, the AI assistant's key, provider and model if you
connected it, and, with a TIN, when the app asked the server about it and
what the server answered. What you entered in the calculator on the site
also stays in your browser, so that the app can offer to take it into the
profile. All of this is erased together with the site data in the browser
settings.

## 3. What the hosting sees

The site runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). When you open the
site, or the app checks for a new version, the browser, as with any site,
passes to Cloudflare your IP address and the technical details of the
request: which page or file it asks for and which browser you have. Your
data from the app is not in these requests, except the TIN in the question
about the trial period (section 2). Calendar apps request the site
the same way if you subscribed to the deadlines calendar.

Cloudflare keeps request records for 31 days, and the site owner can see
them in the Cloudflare dashboard, IP addresses included. We look at them
only to protect the site and to investigate failures, and we link them to
nothing and no one: we do not have your data from the app. Cloudflare also
processes these records under its own privacy policy. The USA is on the
list of countries with adequate protection of personal data kept by the
Personal Data Protection Agency of Armenia.

## 4. Backups and files

You make the backup yourself: it is a file encrypted with your password.
The password is stored nowhere and cannot be recovered. Where to put
a backup, a PDF or an export for your accountant, in email, a messenger or
a cloud, is your choice; the rules of those services apply there.

## 5. Buying a key

To issue a license key the seller needs the TIN (ՀՎՀՀ) of your IE, its
name and a contact to send the key to. Without a TIN no key can be issued:
it is issued to a TIN and does not work with another. The seller receives
the payment details from their bank or from the payment service named on
the “Buy” page.

The seller uses these data only to issue the key, to issue the settlement
document and to meet tax obligations. The basis is the license contract
you make by paying, and tax law. The seller keeps these data and the
purchase correspondence for five years from the quarter of the sale, then
deletes them. They are passed only to the tax authorities when the law
requires it; letters to the seller go through their mail service
Gmail (Google, USA). The seller sends no mailings or advertising
to them.

You can also ask the seller questions in Telegram: the link is on the site
and in “About”. Messages there go through Telegram and under its rules, so
send your TIN (ՀՎՀՀ) and your acceptance of the terms by email.

## 6. Your rights

You may ask the seller which of your data they hold, why and on what basis
they keep it and to whom they may pass it, and demand that data which is
incomplete, inaccurate, outdated or unnecessary for these purposes be
corrected, blocked or deleted. The seller removes the trial period record
for your TIN on the same kind of request. The seller tells you which data they hold
within 5 days of a written request, and corrects or deletes at once or,
if that is impossible, within 3 business days. A refusal is given in
writing, with the reason, within 5 days. Write from the email you bought
with, or with an electronic signature: that is how the seller knows it is
you. You may complain to the authorized body for personal data protection
or to a court.

The data in the app you delete yourself: clearing the site data in the
browser erases everything, so make a backup first.

## 7. Changes

A new edition is published here with its date, and the app tells you about
